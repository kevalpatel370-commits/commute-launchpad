The local launchpad

Resolve the Deptford bottleneck first. Choose your path to I-295 North.

OPTION A / STANDARD

RT 42 North corridor

Check both merge views before committing. Standard, but heavily congested.

OPTION B / ALTERNATE APPROACH

Route 55 North approach

Compare the approach queue with RT 42 before leaving Deptford.

Route 55 joins RT 42. It may improve local access, but it does not independently bypass congestion downstream on RT 42.

OPTION C / LOCAL ESCAPE

Evesham Road local route

Across Magnolia / Somerdale, then connecting local roads toward Woodcrest and I-295 North.

LAUNCH RULES

  1. Check RT 42 at Leaf/Creek Rd. If clear, compare RT 42 N with Route 55 joining RT 42, then continue to I-295 North.
  2. If the shared RT 42 merge is gridlocked, Route 55 will not bypass it. Compare Evesham Road east and connecting local roads toward Woodcrest / I-295 North, or a local approach to Turnpike Exit 3. Confirm the connecting turns in Maps.

Route clarification: Route 55 reaches I-295 through RT 42 and shares its downstream bottleneck. The Evesham bypass requires connecting local roads to Woodcrest; Evesham Road does not directly meet Exit 31. Confirm those turns in Maps.
